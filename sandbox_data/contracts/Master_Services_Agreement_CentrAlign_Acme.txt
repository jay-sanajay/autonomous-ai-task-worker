MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)
Reference Document: CTR-2024-MSA-088
================================================================================

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024 ("Effective Date"),
by and between:

PARTIES:
1. CentrAlign AI Technologies Corp., a Delaware corporation with its principal office at
   100 Innovation Way, Suite 400, San Francisco, CA 94105 ("Client" or "Company"), and
2. Acme Technologies Inc., a California corporation with its principal office at
   500 Enterprise Way, Mountain View, CA 94043 ("Service Provider" or "Vendor").

RECITALS:
Client desires to retain Service Provider to perform autonomous engineering services,
software systems integration, and artificial intelligence task automation workflows.

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES:
Service Provider shall deliver agentic software components, verification critics,
and resilient background workflow workers as described in Statements of Work ("SOW").

2. TERM AND EXPIRY DATE:
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date (January 15, 2024) and shall continue
in full force and effect until its Expiry Date on January 14, 2025 ("Expiry Date"), unless
terminated earlier in accordance with Section 7 herein.
The parties may renew this Agreement by mutual written amendment at least 30 days prior to expiry.

3. FINANCIAL CONSIDERATION & FEES:
The total aggregate value of services under this Master Agreement is capped at
$120,000.00 USD (One Hundred Twenty Thousand US Dollars) per annum, payable in monthly
milestone installments of $10,000.00 USD net 30 days upon delivery and verification.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY & PROPRIETARY RIGHTS:
Each party agrees to maintain in strict confidence all technical, business, and financial
information disclosed by the other party. All custom autonomous agent code, schemas,
and evaluation frameworks developed for Client shall constitute "Work Made for Hire"
and remain the sole property of Client.

5. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION:
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the
State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Any legal suit,
action, or proceeding shall be instituted exclusively in the federal or state courts in Santa Clara County, CA.

6. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE:
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon giving ninety (90) days prior
written notice to the other party.
